# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Nuclear industry researchers asking ordinary language questions about public NRC records. Users need evidence they can inspect and may be frustrated by incomplete coverage or slow answers.

## Product Purpose

Retrieve relevant NRC passages and produce source-linked answers. Preserve document identity, extraction limitations, and the difference between historical evidence and current licensing status.

## Brand Personality

Plain, restrained, direct. Keep the existing DCAF/Uprate-inspired one-box interface requested by the owner.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboards, extra setup controls, vague progress messages, and unsupported claims of complete archive coverage.

## Design Principles

- Keep asking a question simple.
- Show what the service is actually doing.
- Make sources available even when generation fails.
- Explain missing evidence without implying the underlying fact is absent.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard operation, labeled inputs, live status, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support already implemented in the interface.
